Our Story

Locally owned. Built on experience.

Elkhorn AV is owned and run by Matt and Hailey Knadle.

Who we are

Elkhorn AV was started by Matt and Hailey Knadle to give homeowners and builders one local team for audio, video, security, networking and automation. We plan carefully, install cleanly, and stay available after the job is done.

Meet Matt Knadle

Matt brings more than 20 years of hands-on experience in home technology. In that time he has worked on nearly every part of a system, from the first cable pulled through open framing to the final system check and walkthrough with the homeowner.

His work started where the best installs start: in the walls. Years of prewire and trim-out on new construction taught him how a home really goes together, how to work alongside electricians, framers and drywall crews, and how to plan cable runs, speaker locations and equipment spaces so everything is hidden and nothing is an afterthought.

Over the years that grew into the full range of residential systems: home theaters and whole-home audio, alarm and camera systems, structured wiring and Wi-Fi networks, and smart home control. Matt has designed, installed, programmed and serviced these systems, so he knows how they perform years after install, not only on day one.

That hands-on background shapes how Elkhorn AV works. Matt plans around how a family lives, builds it cleanly, and stands behind it with real support after the job is done.

Meet Hailey Knadle

Hailey is co-owner and the dispatcher at Elkhorn AV. She schedules consultations, installs and service calls, and gets the right details in place before anyone steps on a job site.

Behind the scenes, Hailey coordinates the pieces that make a build run on time: lining up meetings with builders and other trades, keeping the crew's schedule organized and routed, handling the paperwork, and following up so nothing is missed.

For homeowners, that means one friendly point of contact who knows your project and returns your calls. For builders, it means a partner who communicates clearly and is easy to work with. Hailey believes great service comes down to follow-through: confirming the details, keeping people informed, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks from the first phone call through the final walkthrough and long after.

Together, Hailey and Matt run Elkhorn AV the way they would want to be treated as customers: organized, responsive, and honest about what comes next.
